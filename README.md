## Hi, I'm the founder of Studio Umbra 👋

I make **cozy mobile games with a big heart**: easy to pick up, hard to put down, and kind to your time.

### 🐾 Currently building: **BlockPaws**
A block puzzle game where every cleared line helps rescue a puppy.

- **Rescue, not just score.** Each level traps a named pup behind cages. Break the cages, bring them home to your shelter.
- **Mud that fights back.** Every few moves, mud creeps toward the pup. Clear it or race it, your choice.
- **Daily Rescue.** One shared puzzle for everyone, every day, with a spoiler-free result card to share with friends.
- **Feel first.** Synthesized sound that climbs with your combo, haptics, slow-motion on the final rescue.

> Status: playable prototype on Android, heading to closed testing.

### 🌗 How we build
- **Test everything.** Game rules live in plain, unit-tested C#, separate from the engine.
- **Measure, don't guess.** Difficulty is tuned with simulated players before real ones ever see it.
- **Respect the player.** Fewer ads, fair deals, no dark patterns.
